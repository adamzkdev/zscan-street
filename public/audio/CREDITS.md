# ZStreet sounds: credits

Every recording below comes from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) and is released under
**Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0, public-domain dedication)**: free to use, modify and redistribute,
attribution not required. Credited here with thanks anyway.

The files were cut, cross-faded into seamless loops (beds) or trimmed (one-shots),
loudness-matched (about -20 LUFS for beds) and encoded as HE-AAC (.m4a).
